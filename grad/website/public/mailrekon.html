What MailRekon Analyses

MailRekon examines multiple layers of a domain's publicly observable email infrastructure. A domain can have a functioning website while its email infrastructure contains outdated, inconsistent or incorrect DNS configuration. MailRekon examines the publicly visible configuration of your email domain and helps you understand where authentication, routing and policy settings may need attention.

Mail DNS

Examine MX records, mail host resolution and related DNS configuration.

Identifies the infrastructure responsible for receiving email.

SPF

Analyse the Sender Policy Framework configuration published by the domain.

Includes policy structure and relevant SPF mechanisms.

DKIM

Inspect discoverable DKIM selectors and their public cryptographic keys.

DKIM discovery depends on selectors published by the domain's sending infrastructure.

DMARC

Analyse the domain's anti-spoofing policy and authentication alignment configuration.

Examines policy, alignment and reporting configuration.

SMTP

Examine the publicly accessible mail transport infrastructure associated with the domain.

Includes connection, SMTP and transport-layer observations.

TLS

Analyse the encryption capabilities presented by mail servers.

Certificate and TLS observations contribute to the transport security assessment.

MTA-STS

Check whether the domain publishes a Mail Transfer Agent Strict Transport Security policy.

DNS and HTTPS policy information are evaluated together.

TLS-RPT

Inspect SMTP TLS reporting configuration and reporting destinations.

Helps identify whether transport failures can be reported.

BIMI

Inspect Brand Indicators for Message Identification records and associated resources.

BIMI is treated as a trust and brand layer rather than a replacement for email authentication.

DANE

Inspect TLSA records where DNS-based authentication of mail transport is available.

DANE results are reported independently from conventional certificate validation.

DNSSEC

Examine DNSSEC-related information relevant to the domain's email security infrastructure.

DNS integrity contributes to the overall security assessment.

Deliverability

Combine observable infrastructure and configuration signals into a practical deliverability assessment.

Deliverability is assessed separately from email security.

MailRekon Methodology

MailRekon analyses publicly observable technical information. It does not require access to a domain's private email accounts.

Public Infrastructure

DNS records, mail hosts, SMTP services and publicly available transport policies are examined.

Security

Authentication, encryption, transport security and domain policy are assessed independently.

Deliverability

Observable infrastructure signals are combined to provide a practical indication of mail delivery readiness.

No Email Required

MailRekon does not need to send an email to the analysed domain in order to perform its initial assessment.
