Discovery Methodology

KlaskerBot performs bounded, read-only Agentic AI protocol discovery, and it does not attempt to purchase any products or services.

Public Signals

Discovery is based on information that a website makes publicly accessible through HTTP endpoints, headers, well-known resources, API descriptions and other protocol-specific signals.

No Transactions

KlaskerBot does not submit payment credentials, place orders, initiate transactions or attempt to complete a purchase.

Based on Evidence

Results are based on observable evidence rather than assumptions about whether a business supports a protocol.

Absence is Not Proof

A protocol marked Not Detected has not been observed at the endpoints examined. Yet, it does not necessarily mean that the business does not support the protocol.

Verification Levels

CONFIRMED

KlaskerBot observed protocol-specific evidence sufficient to confirm the implementation under its current discovery rules.

STRONG

Strong protocol-specific evidence was observed, but the current discovery method does not provide complete confirmation.

POSSIBLE

The observed response resembles a protocol implementation but does not provide sufficient evidence for confirmation.

NOT DETECTED

No qualifying protocol evidence was observed within the bounded discovery process.

UNABLE TO DETERMINE

The available public information was insufficient to make a reliable determination.
