Public Web Observatory

Observatory AITA shows publicly observable characteristics of selected websites, and currently presents two views of the web: traditional Search Engine Optimisation and the emerging field of Answer Engine Optimisation and Agentic eCommerce.


These observations are based on publicly accessible technical evidence and machine-readable signals. All of the data shown may change as websites, platforms and protocols evolve.

What can You Observe?

Observatory AITA verifies what machines can actually discover, understand and use. Traditional SEO focuses primarily on discovery through search engines. AEO extends this mission to answer engines and AI systems. Agentic eCommerce is making commercial capabilities available to software agents.


Observatory AITA

The web is becoming a place where machines do more than retrieve information. They can discover businesses, understand products, interact with services and even perform transactions in your name. Observatory AITA follows this transition through publicly observable technical signals.
