Agentic AI Klasker Frontier

Agentic AI Klasker Frontier is a browser-based space strategy and trade game set across a growing galaxy of planetary systems, colonies and celestial hazards.

The game begins on Earth. Your first objective is to establish a foothold on Mars and begin expanding human activity beyond Earth. From there, exploration, transportation, colonisation and trade will gradually become the foundations of a larger interplanetary economy.

Explore the Galaxy

Discover planetary systems, stars, asteroids, pulsars, supernovae and other extraordinary objects distributed throughout the galaxy.

The galaxy is designed to grow as new systems, destinations and opportunities are introduced.

Colonise New Worlds

Earth is the starting point. Your first major expansion is the colonisation of Mars.

Colonies require transportation, resources and infrastructure before they can become productive settlements.

Trade and Earn KRON

Transport useful resources between worlds and stations. Buy where goods are available and sell where demand creates an opportunity.

KRON earned through successful trade can be invested in ships, cargo capacity, colonies and future expansion.

Build an Armoured Fleet

Different starships serve different purposes. Cargo vessels support trade and colonisation, while faster starships can eventually be used for exploration, security and combat.

Additional starship classes and capabilities will be introduced as the game develops.

Autonomous Agents

Agentic AI Klasker Frontier is designed around a galaxy in which autonomous economic agents can eventually observe markets, select routes, transport goods and pursue their own objectives.

Agentic AI gameplay will expand the economy beyond actions directly controlled by the player.

Space Combat

Combat is an occasional part of the wider game rather than its central purpose. Fighters and other defensive capabilities may eventually protect ships, colonies and trade routes.

Strategic trade and exploration remain at the centre of the game.

How to Play

Begin on Earth, select a destination and develop your first interplanetary route.

1. Start on Earth

Earth is your starting world and the initial centre of your operations.

2. Reach Mars

Establish the first Mars colony by sending the resources and transport required to begin settlement.

3. Develop Trade

Move resources between worlds and look for profitable differences between supply and demand.

4. Expand

Reinvest credits into ships, infrastructure and new destinations as the galaxy becomes more accessible.

An Agentic Economy

Agentic AI Klasker Frontier is intended as an experimental environment for autonomous economic activity. Future versions may allow starships, corporations and other entities to make decisions based on their resources, objectives, market information and relationships with other AI agents.

This creates a living economic system in which opportunities can emerge from the actions of many independent participants rather than from a fixed sequence of "hardwired" missions.
