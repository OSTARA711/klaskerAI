What MailRekon Analyses

MailRekon examines multiple layers of a domain's email infrastructure. A domain can have a functioning website while its email infrastructure contains an outdated or incorrect DNS configuration.


Free Tier — Basic eMail Configuration Assessment

Free Tier analyses publicly observable DNS, authentication and email-security configuration. Where a feature is marked as Basic, MailRekon performs a limited or passive assessment.

MailRekon Methodology

MailRekon analyses publicly observable technical information. It does not require access to a domain's private email accounts.

Public Infrastructure

DNS records, mail hosts, SMTP services and publicly available transport policies are examined.

Security

Authentication, encryption, transport security and domain policy are assessed independently.

Deliverability

Observable infrastructure signals are combined to provide a practical indication of mail delivery readiness.

No Email Required

MailRekon does not require an email address to perform the basic domain analysis.
